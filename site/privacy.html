Privacy Policy

Last updated:

1. What we collect

We collect the minimum needed to run the service: your email address (from Stripe Checkout), your company name if you provide it, billing records, and aggregate usage counters (agent-hours, tasks processed).

2. What we never collect

Your task payloads, support tickets, code, documents, prompts and model outputs stay on your infrastructure. Federated learning reports contain eight numeric fields only; the API rejects any other field, and rejects string values longer than 128 characters to close side channels.

3. Why we process it

To deliver the service (contract), to bill you correctly (contract and legal obligation), and to keep the platform secure (legitimate interest). We do not sell personal data and we do not use it to train models.

4. Processors

Stripe (payments), your chosen hosting providers, and email delivery for transactional messages. Each processor is bound by its own data processing agreement. Payment card data never reaches our servers.

5. Retention

Billing records are kept as long as tax law requires (typically 10 years). API keys are stored only as SHA-256 hashes. Service logs are kept for 30 days. Deleting your account removes your keys and tenant metadata; your local data is yours to delete.

6. Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. Write to privacy@braincore.pro and we respond within 30 days.

7. Security

Peer messages are HMAC-signed; task payloads are encrypted with AES-256-GCM keys derived from your cluster secret; the audit log is hash-chained so tampering is detectable. Transport between nodes should additionally run inside WireGuard or a private network.

8. Honest limitations

The federation tenant_hash is a salted hash used for grouping reports. It is a pseudonymisation measure, not legal anonymisation, and we say so plainly instead of implying otherwise.
